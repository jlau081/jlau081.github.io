Jackie Lau

Psychology Student · Aspiring Children’s Behavioral Therapist

Hello! My name is Jackie Lau, and I am a Psychology student at the University of California, Riverside, expecting to graduate in 2027. I am interested in understanding how children grow, learn, and respond to the world around them. My goal is to become a children's behavioral therapist and help young people develop the tools they need to navigate emotional and behavioral challenges.

I chose psychology because of my own experiences growing up in a separated family household. I have seen firsthand how difficulties with coparenting can affect a child's development and upbringing. Those experiences motivated me to learn more about family relationships, emotional well-being, and the support children need as they grow. They also helped me recognize the importance of listening without judgment and treating each person's circumstances with care.

I believe children deserve to feel safe, supported, and understood. In my future career, I hope to help children build confidence, express their feelings, and practice healthy ways of responding to challenges. I am particularly interested in how family environments influence childhood development and how caring adults can make a meaningful difference. Studying psychology is an important step toward developing the knowledge and skills necessary for that work.

Alongside my education, I have worked in several service and administrative roles. As a tennis academy secretary and invoice administrator, I gained experience supporting daily operations and handling administrative responsibilities. As a nail salon receptionist, I worked in a customer-facing setting where communication and organization were important. I also worked as a Korean BBQ server, a fast-paced role that required attentiveness and teamwork. These positions exposed me to different people and situations and helped me appreciate the value of patience, flexibility, and clear communication.

Although my previous jobs are in different industries, I see connections between those experiences and my future goals. Working with customers and coworkers has helped me practice listening, responding thoughtfully, and staying organized when multiple tasks need attention. I hope to carry those habits into my future work with children and families, while continuing to learn the specialized approaches used in behavioral therapy.

At UC Riverside, I want to deepen my understanding of developmental psychology and seek opportunities to learn more about working with children. I am excited to explore relevant volunteer opportunities, research, and future professional training. I know that becoming a behavioral therapist requires continued education and hands-on experience, and I am committed to taking those steps thoughtfully.

I value kindness, humility, and personal growth. I try to become a better person than I was the day before, both in my academic work and in how I treat others. This website is a place to share my education, professional experience, and developing interests. As I continue my journey, I look forward to adding new projects and achievements that reflect what I learn along the way.
